# BrgyLink Resident Quick Guide

**Barangay Bagong Pag-asa**
**Version 2.2.0 | For residents using the BrgyLink mobile app**

This quick guide shows the resident services currently available in BrgyLink. It is designed to be read on a phone or printed as a short reference. For changing information such as office schedules, fees, or officials, check official BrgyLink announcements or contact the barangay office.

The interactive version is available at [mission17-website/public/infographic-manual.html](mission17-website/public/infographic-manual.html).

## What You Can Do

| Service | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Document Requests | Request a Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Indigency, or Certificate of Residency. |
| Blotter Reports | File and follow an incident report. |
| Announcements | Read official barangay updates in the app. |
| BrgyLink AI | Get guidance about BrgyLink services and account steps. |

## Start Here

1. Download and open BrgyLink.
2. Create an account with your real name, email, and password.
3. Enter the six-digit code sent to your email. Check Inbox, Spam, and Junk folders.
4. Complete your profile and submit clear front and back photos of your valid ID.
5. Wait for barangay approval before signing in.

**Keep your account safe.** Never share your password, OTP, or reset link with another person or with BrgyLink AI.

## Request a Document

1. Open **Document Requests** from the home screen.
2. Choose Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Indigency, or Certificate of Residency.
3. State the purpose of the request and attach the required valid ID.
4. Submit, then check **My Requests** for updates.

Your request may move through these statuses:

`Pending` -> `Processing` -> `Ready for Pickup` -> `Completed`

Go to the barangay office only when the app indicates that the document is ready. Bring your valid ID when collecting it.

## File a Blotter Report

1. Open **Blotter Reports** and tap **File New Report**.
2. Select the incident type and provide a specific location.
3. Enter the date and time when the incident happened.
4. Write a clear factual narrative. Attach available photo evidence.
5. Submit and monitor the report in **My Blotter Reports**.

For an emergency, call **911**. Do not use the app as a substitute for immediate emergency response.

## Use BrgyLink AI

Open **Assistant** and choose a suggested question or type your own question in English, Tagalog, Pangasinan, or Ilocano. It can guide you on document requests, blotter reports, registration, OTP, password resets, announcements, and supported app features.

BrgyLink AI gives guidance only. Confirm fees, schedules, officials, and other changing details through official announcements or the barangay office.

## If Something Does Not Work

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| OTP is missing | Recheck the email address, Inbox, Spam, and Junk. Wait for the resend timer, then request a new code. |
| OTP is expired | Request a new code and use only the latest one. |
| Account cannot sign in after registration | Make sure the barangay has approved the submitted account. |
| A form will not submit | Check your connection, review required fields and attachments, then try again. |
| You need an official answer | Check BrgyLink announcements or contact the barangay office. |

## Before You Submit

- Use your real resident information.
- Review text, attachments, date, and location before submitting.
- Use clear photos of valid IDs and evidence.
- Do not submit false or misleading blotter reports.
- Keep your phone notifications enabled for BrgyLink updates when available.
