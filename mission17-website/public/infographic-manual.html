01 / Start here

Create an account before using resident services.

Have an active email address and clear photos of your valid ID ready before you register.

STEP 01

Create account

Enter your real name, username, email address, and password.

STEP 02

Verify email

Enter the six-digit code sent to your email. Check Spam and Junk too.

STEP 03

Complete profile

Provide your contact and address details, then choose the correct Purok.

STEP 04

Submit valid ID

Upload clear front and back ID photos. Wait for barangay approval.

03 / Blotter

File a factual report with the details officials need.

Use the in-app report for non-emergency incidents. For an emergency, call 911 instead of waiting for an app response.

  • Open Blotter Reports and tap File New Report.
  • Select the incident type and write a specific location: purok, street, landmark, or house number.
  • Use the date and time when the incident happened, not when you are filing the report.
  • Write a clear factual narrative and attach available photo evidence.
  • Check My Blotter Reports for updates and Lupon hearing details when scheduled.
04 / Assistant

Ask BrgyLink AI for guidance, not for confidential information.

The assistant can answer supported questions in English, Tagalog, Pangasinan, and Ilocano.

05 / Need help

Use these checks when something does not work.

Most account and submission issues can be resolved by checking the required details, attachment, and connection first.

Account or form issue

Missing or expired OTP: Check Inbox, Spam, and Junk, wait for the resend timer, then request a new code.

Cannot sign in after registration: Confirm that the account has been approved by the barangay.

Form will not submit: Check the internet connection, required fields, and attachments, then try again.

Emergency

Call 911
Do not wait for a chatbot answer, app notification, or online report when immediate help is needed.
